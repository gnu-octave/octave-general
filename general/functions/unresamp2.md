<dt class="deftypefn def-line" id="index-unresamp2"><span class="category-def">Function File: </span><span><code class="def-type">[<var class="var">xs</var>, <var class="var">ys</var>] =</code> <strong class="def-name">unresamp2</strong> <code class="def-code-arguments">(<var class="var">x</var>, <var class="var">y</var>, <var class="var">n</var>)</code></span></dt>
<dd><p>Perform a uniform resampling of a planar curve.
 The arrays <var class="var">x</var> and <var class="var">y</var> specify x and y coordinates of the points of the curve.
 On return, the same curve is approximated by <var class="var">xs</var>, <var class="var">ys</var> that have length <var class="var">n</var>
 and the distances between successive points are approximately equal.
 </p></dd></dl>
